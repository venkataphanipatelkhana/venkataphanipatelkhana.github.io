About

I am a Distinguished Engineer and IEEE Senior Member with more than 19 years of experience across enterprise API platforms, distributed systems, developer portals, decentralized gateways, API security, and agentic AI architecture.

My current work and independent research focus on how enterprises can govern AI agents through identity, context, bounded authority, runtime authorization, MCP gateways, enterprise graphs, human approvals, observability, and control-plane architectures.

My writing and speaking connect lessons from the API platform era with the emerging agent-first enterprise: APIs expose capabilities, MCP connects agents to tools and context, and agentic systems add the ability to plan, decide, delegate, and act.
